Meet Jeff

Why I'm running

I live on Henry Avenue, and I've called the Milwaukee area home for more than 20 years. I love this part of Cudahy: quiet streets and neighbors who look out for each other.

Lately I've watched our city stretch to make ends meet. In January 2026 the Common Council closed a $1.1 million budget gap with a new $25 wheel tax. Our streets and alleys need work. And when I looked at the last District 5 election, voters had only one name to choose from.

That's when I decided to step up. For 23 years I've worked in cybersecurity at Northwestern Mutual. My job is to read the fine print, ask the questions nobody else asks, and catch small problems before they become expensive ones. City Hall needs more of that, and District 5 deserves a choice: someone who asks for this job and then works for it.

This isn't about anyone else. It's about our block.

What I'll work on

Protect what's ours

My whole career has been about protection. Here's what that looks like for District 5. I'll keep adding to this list as I hear from you at the door.

Your tax dollars

Before the city asks residents for more, I'll read the budget line by line and push to find savings first. Every new fee should come with a clear answer to one question: what did we cut before we asked you?

Your streets and services

Roads, alleys, trash pickup and the drop-off site are what residents feel every day. I'll push for a public list of street repairs, ranked and updated, so you can see when your block is coming up and why.

Your city's systems and data

Small cities are prime targets for ransomware. Baltimore's recovery from one attack cost about $18 million. I'll push for low-cost basics, like staff training and a tested recovery plan, that keep a cyberattack from becoming a tax bill.

Your neighborhood

Vacant and neglected properties drag down a whole block. I'll work with city staff to get problem properties fixed, filled, or cleaned up, and I'll follow up until they are.
