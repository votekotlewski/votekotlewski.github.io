Meet Jeff

Why I'm running

Cudahy has been part of my family's story for generations. My great-grandparents and grandparents called Cudahy home, as did my mother, Bonnie Bessler-Kotlewski. My grandfather, Bud Bessler, served this community as an alderperson and school board member. As an artist, he also helped bring the Cudahy War Memorial to life through his design, a contribution to the community that filled him with tremendous pride. Through his example, he showed me the importance of giving back to the community you call home.

I have lived in Milwaukee County my entire life and have called Cudahy home for more than 23 years, living in the same home throughout that time. My grandfather's example of service, commitment, and pride in Cudahy has stayed with me throughout my life. Today, I want to honor those values in my own way, not simply by remembering my family's history here, but by stepping forward to serve the community that has become such an important part of my own life and my family's story.

I'm running for District 5 Alderperson because I want to carry those values forward in my own way, listen to and represent our residents, and give back to a community that has meant so much to my family and me. I want to help preserve what makes Cudahy special while working together to make our city even stronger for the generations that follow.

I would be honored to earn your trust and your vote as we work together to build an even stronger Cudahy.

What I'll work on

Protect what's ours

Much of my career has focused on protecting what matters, managing risk, solving problems, and being accountable for results. I want to bring that same mindset to District 5 by protecting taxpayer dollars, the services we rely on, our city's systems, and the neighborhoods we call home. And this list will continue to grow as I listen to residents and learn what matters most to you.

Your tax dollars

Every tax dollar deserves to be treated with care. My professional experience includes working with budgets, vendors, and contracts, where asking questions, understanding the details, and looking for value matter. Before asking residents to pay more, I believe we should understand where the money is going, look for responsible savings and efficiencies, and be transparent about why additional spending is necessary.

Your streets and services

Good local government starts with the basics. Well-maintained streets and alleys, reliable trash collection, and accessible city services directly affect residents' quality of life. I'll advocate for clear priorities and greater transparency around street and infrastructure repairs so residents can better understand what work is planned, when it's expected, and how those decisions are made.

Your city's systems and data

Protecting Cudahy also means protecting the technology and information our city depends on. Cybersecurity may not always be visible, but a cyberattack can disrupt essential city services and ultimately cost taxpayers. With more than two decades of experience in Information Technology and Cybersecurity, I understand the importance of managing technology risk and preparing for the unexpected. I'll advocate for practical safeguards, employee awareness, reliable backups and tested recovery plans, and responsible technology investments that help keep city services available while protecting taxpayer dollars.

Your neighborhood

Strong neighborhoods depend on residents, property owners, and the city all doing their part. Vacant, neglected, and poorly maintained properties can affect an entire block. I'll work with residents and city staff to understand problem properties, pursue appropriate solutions, and follow through so concerns don't simply disappear after they're reported.

My promise to District 5

You'll always know how to reach me

I'll show up

I'll come to Common Council meetings prepared, informed, and ready to represent District 5. But showing up means more than attending meetings. It means listening to residents, asking questions, doing the homework, and being present in our community. I'm starting that work now, before Election Day.

I'll answer

When you reach out, you deserve a response. I'll personally read messages from District 5 residents and make responsiveness a priority. If your question requires more research or help from the city, I'll follow through and keep you informed until I can provide an answer or a clear next step.

I'll listen

Good representation starts with listening before deciding. I'll create opportunities to hear directly from District 5 residents, whether that's at your door, by phone or email, at neighborhood conversations, or simply when we cross paths around Cudahy. My commitment is to listen, understand your concerns, and make sure your voice is heard.

I'll report back

After every Common Council meeting, I'll provide a clear recap of the decisions that matter to District 5, how I voted, why I voted that way, and what happens next.
